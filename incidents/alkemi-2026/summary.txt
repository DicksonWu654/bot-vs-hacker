Alkemi Earn (eth-vault-copiers)
The 43.45 ETH Alkemi ETH-vault drain (block 24626979) was never front-run: all 7 later txs running the self-liquidation call sequence landed 1 to 444 blocks later, the best taking 0.72 ETH of leftovers. Yoink was not involved.
Two different pools of the same Alkemi self-liquidation bug were drained by different parties. (1) ETH vault: the DHL-listed attacker 0x0ed1c01b took 43.45395 ETH at block 24626979 (12:10:11Z); Yoink never touched this pool and no copier beat him (first copy 12 s later, 0.7173 ETH of leftovers 15 min later). (2) WBTC pool (0.29980756 WBTC): an unrelated EOA 0x46c403e3 sent a deploy+attack tx at block 24627180 idx 226 that reverted out of gas; Yoink's tx at block 24627181 idx 0 contains the same 16 state-changing Alkemi calls (+2 withdraws) from its own contract and took 0.29925557 WBTC (10.2359 ETH gross, 10.2256 ETH to the coinbase). That is a replay after a failed, already-mined tx, not a pre-emption of a pending one. The message sender 0x8366d87b ('Why did you leak my attack transaction to yoink?') is a third party whose own WBTC attack tx (0xd4ec3e34, block 24627185) landed after Yoink's and found the pool empty; its program differs from Yoink's, so Yoink did not replay it, but my fork replay shows it would have taken the whole WBTC pool had it landed first, so Yoink did take the pool it was about to drain. Whether Yoink saw that tx (the 'leak') cannot be shown: none of the 10 Alkemi-touching txs is in mempool-dumpster.
