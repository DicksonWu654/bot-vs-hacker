MintSwap vMINT/ETH pool, Ethereum, 2024-04-01. Verdict: related-not-frontrun, race_type bot-was-first-exploiter (not a headline case). Slug mintswap-2024.

The pool 0xd8d5b077 went live at 12:00:59 UTC with 66.4 ETH and 3,997,707 vMINT. Its swapExactTokensForETH prices a sale with getAmountIn instead of getAmountOut, so selling about half the pool's tokens returns almost all its ETH. For 7 h 49 min the pool only saw buys (6 user buys, 2 small buys by a pool-vs-Uniswap arbitrage searcher, 2 reverted buys). At 19:49:35 Yoink's tx 0xd2337efb (block 19563164, index 0) flash-loaned USDC, bought 1.88M vMINT on Uniswap V2 and sold it to the pool for 67.86 ETH of the pool's 69.54. The next block's tx 0x29edec50 bought 4.45M vMINT back from the emptied pool for 5.98 ETH and sold it on V2 (60.55 ETH out). Four more Yoink txs (blocks 19563183-190) re-hit ETH that other arbs had put back. In six blocks the builder 'I can haz block?' took 110.80 ETH of Yoink coinbase payments, refunded 101.73 ETH and passed 9.20 ETH to proposers, which reconciles with Yoink's own message (profit 101.8 ETH, about 9 ETH to validators; our estimate 101.80 ETH net of gas). No separate attacker exists: the two txs the MintSwap team cites are Yoink's. The only third-party tx Yoink pre-empted is an innocent user's pending 5,991 vMINT sell (public mempool 19:49:25.637, 9.4 s before the block), which reverted; a trigger is plausible but unproven. After the team's on-chain message (10% bounty offer) Yoink returned 91.62 ETH to the LP 0x5b03 on 2024-04-02 14:03:47.

Three most interesting facts:
1. Yoink's tx is the first call to the sell path in the pool's history; the pending user sell was the only public call to it, and Yoink's router deadline (build time + 3600 s = 19:49:25) falls in the same second, though its other five txs show the same 0-2 s-after-block build rhythm.
2. The refund data reproduces Yoink's "101.8 profit / ~9 ETH to validators" statement almost exactly (101.73 ETH refunded, 9.20 ETH to proposers).
3. The 91.62 ETH return exceeds the 66.4 ETH the LP deposited and the 69.49 ETH the pool lost; DeFiHackLabs has no MintSwap entry.
