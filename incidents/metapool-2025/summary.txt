Meta Pool
Yoink was the first to exploit Meta Pool's mpETH mint() bug (50.77 ETH gross, block 22722394), 50 min before the attacker's first test run and 1 h 44 min before its main tx; it returned its net 45.79 ETH and received a 5 ETH reward.
Yoink's tx 0x4f43fc6d (block 22722394, index 0, 2025-06-17T06:18:59Z) is the FIRST tx ever to use the mpETH mint-without-deposit bug; the human attacker's first exploit tx (0x691c8070, EOA 0xd08559ce, block 22722643) landed 50 min 00 s later and its main tx (0x57ee419a, block 22722911) 1 h 44 min later. Nothing of the attacker's was pending when Yoink landed: the main-tx EOA 0x48f1d0f5 held only 0.00083 ETH until the first test run paid it 1.64 ETH at 07:08:59Z (its 29.1M-gas main tx needs ~0.03 ETH), the older EOA 0xd08559ce had made no Meta Pool call before 07:08:59Z, and none of the attacker's txs ever appears in the public mempool archive. So the press wording 'Yoink front-ran the attacker' is wrong in the race sense: Yoink independently found/used the bug, the attacker (or whoever built the second tx) came afterwards and reproduced the same call grammar with their own contracts and a Balancer flash loan. Yoink then returned its net 45.79 ETH to the Meta Pool admin EOA and received a 5 ETH reward; the attacker returned nothing.
