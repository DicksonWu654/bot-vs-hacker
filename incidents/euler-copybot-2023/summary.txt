Euler Finance copy bot (2023-03-13) - verdict: confirmed-onchain, sub-type "copy-of-contract front-run that captured nothing" (in-block evidence only; no mempool archive)

What the chain shows. The real attacker, EOA 0xB2698C2D (funded by Tornado Cash), deployed its exploit contract 0x036cec1a at block 16817993 (08:50:23Z, tip 3 gwei). One block later a fresh EOA 0x5F259D0b deployed a contract (tx 0x725db76c, tip 37 gwei) whose 17,115-byte creation calldata is byte-identical to the attacker's except four copies of the attacker's 20-byte address swapped for its own. The hard-coded cash-out wallet 0xb66cd966 was left in. In block 16817996, position 0 (tx 0xc310a0af, 37.9 gwei tip, no coinbase payment) it called the DAI-market entry point of its copy: Aave flash loan 30M DAI, 38.9M DAI out of Euler, 30.027M repaid, 8,877,507.35 DAI left in the copy. That is the first exploit tx of the whole hack. The hacker's own first call (WETH variant, block 16818013) came 17 blocks later and ran out of gas; it never called the DAI entry point at all. The copier called withdraw() itself at block 16818823 (11:38Z) and the contract sent all 8,877,507.35 DAI to the hard-coded 0xb66cd966. Calls to the copies by other addresses reverted. It spent about 0.61 ETH in gas, and the 12:08Z on-chain message calls itself an MEV bot owner.

Most interesting facts
1. Bytecode diff: exactly 4 x 20 bytes differ; the copier changed the owner but not the payout address.
2. It was not a replay of a pending attacker call: it copied the attacker's deployment and used an unused entry point, so the "front-run" is of the whole exploit, not one tx.
3. Position 0 and a 37.9 gwei tip are visible on chain (0.0739 ETH, 78% of the 0.09425 ETH Flashbots winning bid). Whether submission used the public mempool or a bundle cannot be determined: in-block evidence only; no mempool archive.
Caveat: the copier may be the attacker's own second account. It was a fresh single-purpose EOA, retained the attacker's cash-out wallet, and captured nothing. Its own on-chain message is the only claim it is a bot. Evidence strength: medium; excluded from the headline.
