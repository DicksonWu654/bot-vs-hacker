Balancer v2 Composable Stable Pool rounding exploit (mainnet leg)
Balancer v2 mainnet: whitehat Bitfinding replayed the attacker's exploit input one block after it landed, rescued about 260 ETH-equivalent in this tx, and returned it to the DAO; a 10% bounty was paid on 2026-01-15.
On the Ethereum mainnet leg no bot or whitehat front-ran an attacker tx. Every replay of the attacker calls (Bitfinding 0x5af00b07 = the whitehat that returned ~$964K; plus searcher/copycat EOAs) lands in a LATER block than the attacker tx on the same pool: Bitfinding 1 block after A1 (23717398 vs 23717397), 4 blocks after A3, 3 blocks after A9. The call data is a confirmed adaptation (A1 creation input vs Bitfinding input: 3 of 983 32-byte words differ, all address constants), so the "front-ran the attacker" wording in the Balancer post-mortem (as quoted by AMBCrypto) is a post-inclusion replay/rescue of the residual pool balances, not a pre-emption of a pending attacker tx. A1 and most attacker txs never appeared in the public mempool; the only public pending ones (A8-A9b) were not pre-empted. Same-block activity before/after the attacker is arbitrage backrunning (0xc0ffee445a22 at idx 2 paying 20.6 ETH to the builder).
