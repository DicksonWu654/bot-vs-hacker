Alkimiya SilicaPools, 2025-03-28 (Ethereum) - verdict: confirmed-onchain front-run, with a return and a bounty

The original attacker is EOA 0xf6ffba5c...2b6e (Tornado-funded in 2023). Over 52 minutes (blocks 22146080 to 22146338) it iterated through four contract designs and deployed the final helpers: a fake pool-"index" contract 0x9188738a (block 22146328) and the attack contract 0x69af47f3 (block 22146335). Its attack tx 0xc8875004 (Morpho 10 WBTC flash loan, then collateralizedMint with a uint128 cast bug, then redeemShort) landed in block 22146341 and reverted. The pool had been emptied one block earlier.

Yoink's tx 0x9b9a6dd0 sits at position 0 of block 22146340. Decoded with the bot analysis grammar, it is a Morpho flashLoan with a callback script of 8 CALLs. Those match the attacker's call tree one for one: the same 7 SilicaPools/index calls, with only the caller contract address and the timestamp words changed (0x67e6c18b vs 0x67e6c197). Yoink even reuses the attacker's own deployed index contract. A debug_traceCall of the attacker tx on the block-22146339 state succeeds with exactly +114,015,390 sat WBTC, which is what Yoink took and swapped for 50.885 ETH.

Auction: Yoink's builder "I can haz block?" escalated 0.54 to 2.576 ETH in 70 ms (-1114 to -1048 ms) and won at -891 ms. Yoink bribed 50.834 ETH (999 per mille) to the coinbase. The builder refunded 48.278 ETH to Yoink in the same block and paid the proposer only 2.576 ETH.

Funds: on 30 March Yoink sent 48.3287 ETH to a Safe linked to Alkimiya (inferred). The Safe sent 6.3287 ETH (13%) back the next day. The net return is 42.0 ETH.

Three facts: (1) DeFiHackLabs lists Yoink's tx as the attack, but the header does name 0xF6ff.. as the original attacker; Verichains and Olympix describe Yoink's tx as the attack. (2) The attacker spent 0.0158 ETH on gas and got nothing. (3) The attacker's txs are absent from mempool-dumpster, so first-seen and how Yoink learned of the attack are UNKNOWN.
