Saddle Finance, 2022-04-30 - verdict: related-not-frontrun (whitehat rescue of remaining funds)

The attacker (EOA 0x63341ba9..., Tornado-funded at block 14684286) deployed three attack contracts and sent three swap-loop txs against the sUSDv2 metapool. Attempt 1 (block 14684307) took 3,375.67 WETH. Attempt 2 (block 14684432) reverted out of gas on its last WETH transfer, leaving the funds in the pool. Two blocks and 25 seconds later, BlockSec's account 0x811929c3... deployed a contract and called it in the same block (14684434, indexes 0-1, 202.5 gwei). Its call tree is frame-for-frame identical to the attacker's (403 frames, 9 swaps) and carries attempt 2's exact swap amounts; the rescue contract's bytecode differs from attacker contract 2 in only 20 bytes. It swept 1,356.73 WETH and returned it to Saddle in tx 0x22efe474... (block 14686088, ~6.5 h later). The attacker then redeployed and took another 557.09 WETH at block 14684518.

This is a replay-of-a-reverted-attempt rescue, not a race against a pending attacker tx: no attacker tx is shown to be pending at 14684434 (the attacker's next contract was deployed 76 blocks later), and the rescue came after, not before, the attacker's attempt 2 was mined. No other generalized bot replayed the swap loop (only three txs ever emitted it). In-block evidence only; no mempool or Flashbots bundle data for this date.

Three facts: (1) rescue replays attempt 2's parameters from a different address, with a 20-byte-diff clone of the attacker's contract; (2) it landed 2 blocks after the revert, before the attacker's retry, which therefore got only 557 WETH; (3) all 1,356.73 WETH was returned.
