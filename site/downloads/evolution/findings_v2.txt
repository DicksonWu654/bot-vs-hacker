HEADLINE
The attacker-side trend survives the correction, and part of it gets sharper; the 'bribes and gas' half does not.  v1 mixed MEV bots, whitehats and
non-attack txs into the attacker sample: of the 315 analysed exploit rows, 34 had no attacker-sent tx at all and drop out (28 bot-only rows, 1 whitehat, 5 unknown-sender or non-attack)
(35 rows had a first listed tx that was not an attacker's: those 34 plus 1 mixed row whose first tx was a bot's but which also lists an attacker tx, so it stays and is measured on that tx).  The corrected attacker sample has 281 rows
(285 counting 4 unknown-sender rows in the sensitivity run).
Visibility: since Aug 2023 9 of 202 resolved attacker txs (4.5%, 95% 2.4-8.2%) were seen in the public mempool, against
62% of ordinary txs in the same blocks (86% in 2023 H2, 48-59% in 2025-26): attackers are about 14x less visible than their neighbours (v1: 4.4%).
Mechanics: constructor-only exploits 0% (E1) -> 9.1% (E2) -> 4.9% (E3) -> 37% (E4); 16% (2024) -> 62% (2025) -> 38% (2026 YTD).  Median fresh deploy-to-attack gap 13.0 min/31.8 min (E1/E2) -> 5.1 min -> 2.4 min;
76% of 2025 exploits left a bot no watchable window (v1: 71%).
What does NOT survive: the 'attackers bribe builders' story.  Attacker coinbase payments are 7.3% (E3) and 8.4% (E4), not 11% and 16%, with no rise from 2023 to 2024;
the big payments (Makina 1,299 ETH, DeFiPlaza 66, Alkimiya 51, Kelp 19, Earning.Farm 19, NUM 10) were MEV bots paying builders.  The median tx index no longer falls monotonically
(E3 12, E4 16.5; 2024 21.5 vs 2025 7.5), because the top-of-block rows were bots.
Limit unchanged: exposure cannot be measured directly before 2023-08-07; before that date it is 'in-block evidence only'.

STRONGEST EVIDENCE OF EVOLUTION (v1's seven points, re-checked)
1. Visibility gap -- HOLDS.  Attacker txs seen in the public mempool before inclusion: 9 of 202 (4.5%; v1 10 of 227) vs 86% of
   neighbouring txs in 2023 H2 and 55%/48%/52% in 2025 H2/2026.  The 9 public ones: OrbitChain, ParticleTrade, PeapodsFinance, Raft, Unilend, WiseLending02, WiseLending03, XBridge, kTAF; seen a median 8.9 s before the block timestamp (n=9).
   By half-year attacker public/resolved: 2023H2 3/37, 2024H1 5/35, 2024H2 0/28, 2025H1 1/31, 2025H2 0/19, 2026H1 0/26, 2026H2 0/26.  The bot rows removed were all private, so the shift is small; the control comparison is unchanged.
2. Constructor shift -- HOLDS, a little stronger.  0% (2020-21), 7.1% (2022), 6.1% (2023), 16% (2024), 55% (2025H1), 74% (2025H2), 27%/50% (2026H1/H2).  The 2024 -> 2025 jump is outside the intervals ([8.7, 26.4] vs [48.2, 74.1]).  No bot row was a constructor exploit, so removing bots raises the share.
3. The window closes -- HOLDS.  Median fresh gap: 13.0 min/31.8 min (E1/E2) -> 5.1 min (E3) -> 72 s (2025, n=13) -> 60 s (2026, n=24).
   Same-block deploy 46% of own-contract attacks in 2025 and 33% in 2026.  The E2 median rose from 23 to 31.8 min because Nomad's copycat drains left the sample; E1/E2 are tiny.
4. Gas competition stopped -- PARTLY HOLDS.  Median priority fee 118 gwei (2021) -> 3.0 (2023) -> 1.75 (2024) -> 0.48 (2025) -> 0.10 (2026), still falling, but ordinary tips fell too.
   The tx-index half does NOT hold: median index 51.5 (E2), 12 (E3), 16.5 (E4); by year 2022 8.5, 2023 13.5, 2024 21.5, 2025 7.5, 2026 8
   (v1 E4 8.5): bots were the ones sitting at index 0.  Zero-tip-included is 3% of E4 attackers (v1 10.5%): it was a bot signature.
5. Bribes appear -- DOES NOT HOLD as an attacker trend.  Attacker coinbase payments: 0% (E1), 4.5% (E2, 1 row), 7.3% (E3), 8.4% (E4); by year 2022 7.1%, 2023 7.6%, 2024 4.7%, 2025 10%, 2026 12%.
   Intervals overlap everywhere (E3 [3.4, 15.1], E4 [5.1, 13.7]).  Hopelend 264 ETH and Anyswap 34 ETH stay; Makina, DeFiPlaza and the rest are bot payments.  A mild rise in 2025-26 (10-12%) is within noise.
6. What did not change -- HOLDS, cleaner.  Flash loan 55% (E3) / 42% (E4) / by year 36%, 59%, 38%, 44%, 44%; unverified attack contracts 100%-98%.
   ORIGIN check: v1 showed a rise to 40.6% in E4; attacker-only it is 17%/27%/29%/30% (E1-E4): the bot contracts carried the guard.
7. chain scan copycat candidates -- HOLDS (still no resolvable trend).  0/22 (E2), 1/82 (E3), 2/166 (E4): FixedTokenBSwap, Kashi, OMPxContract.  Nomad (E2) and RsETHSafeModule dropped out of the
   attacker sample (unknown-sender and bot-only rows); candidates are candidates, not confirmations.

EXTERNAL COMPARISON
- BACKRUNNER (arXiv 2409.06213): 17 of 158 attack txs visible in the public mempool, Jan 2023 - May 2024 (10.8%).  Ours, exploit level, attacker-only, overlapping measurable window Aug 2023 - May 2024:
  8 of 67 public (11.9%) (v1, all senders: 8 of 75, 10.7%).  They still agree.  Their 'bots intercepted 18/158' is not comparable to chain scan's candidates; chain scan finds 0 medium/strong
  candidates among the 96 attacker-only exploits dated Jan 2023 - May 2024 (v1: 0 of 109 all-sender rows).  BACKRUNNER saw 4 constructor attack txs in 1.5 years; we count constructor exploits per exploit, not per tx.
- APE (Qin et al.): 10 of 12 whitehat attack txs private (83%).  Our dumpster-era attackers are 95.5% private against 62% for neighbours,
  so private submission is the norm for adversaries too.  The one whitehat row (BlockSec, Paraspace) predates the archive and cannot be compared; the 9 c0ffeebabe rows are bot-side and unanalysed.

CAVEATS
1. Sender class is evidence-ranked, not ground truth.  High confidence = the address is named in an incident file, literature and anonymous-sender research, or (c0ffeebabe) the DHL sender;
   low confidence = a heuristic (R1/R1b/R2/R3 in definitions.rule_order).  Low-confidence bots: CoW, GHT/PANDORA, NUM, VRug, unverified_03f9.
   The sensitivity run only adds back unknown-sender rows; we did not run a variant that returns the low-confidence bot rows to attacker (their weight is the 'low-confidence bots' group above:
   E4 bribe share 16.3% -> 14.7% and zero-tip 10.5% -> 8.7% if only they are removed from v1).  A busy account (>=1000 prior txs) is not automatically a bot: some 'unknown' rows could be experienced exploiters.
