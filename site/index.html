    What a race looks like

    An attacker sends a transaction that drains a contract. Before that transaction lands, it exists in three places at once: in the public mempool, if it was sent there; in the builder auction that runs inside every twelve-second slot; and, once the slot is won, in the block.

    A bot that spots the attack can copy it, swap in its own address, and bid for the block. The winning bid is the only thing the proposer sees. Everything else in this piece is reconstructed from saved responses: relay bid streams, mempool archives, blocks and traces. Pick any race below and replay it.
