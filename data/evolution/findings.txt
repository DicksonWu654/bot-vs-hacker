evolution study: Has the dark forest evolved? (attacker-side longitudinal measurement)

Generated 2026-10-21 by scripts/evolution.py (stages: txs, traces, mempool, calibrate, analyze).
Data: per_exploit.json (one row per exploit, every measured field plus raw_refs into site/raw/), by_era.json (overall, by era, by year,
dumpster-era by quarter), calibration.json (ground-truth checks of the heuristics and of the mempool archive's own coverage drift).

HEADLINE
Yes, and the change is on the attacker's side. In 2020 every exploit we can see was an ordinary public transaction. Since Aug 2023,
when exposure can be measured directly, 217 of 227 resolved exploits (95.6%) never touched the public mempool, while ordinary
transactions in the same blocks were seen 86% of the time in 2023 H2 and about 50% in 2025-26 (attackers are roughly 11-16x less
visible than their neighbours). The mechanics changed too: exploits that run entirely inside a contract constructor rose from 0% (2020-21)
to 6% (2022-23), 12% (2024), 58% (2025), 37% (2026 YTD), and the median deploy-to-attack gap fell from 13-23 minutes to about 1 minute.
By 2025, 71% of exploits left a bot no watchable window (constructor, internal CREATE, or deploy in the same block as the attack).
Limit: exposure cannot be measured directly before 2023-08-07 (Blocknative archive host unreachable; blocks.flashbots.net returns HTTP 410).
Before that date: "in-block evidence only".

SAMPLE
DeFiHackLabs mainnet entries (chain scan exploits_mainnet.json): 365. 320 have a resolvable mainnet tx, 5 are duplicate listings of one incident
(excluded) -> 315 analysed. 9 entries list a tx sent by c0ffeebabe.eth; those txs were never fetched or traced (publication policy): BiFi,
SecuredFi, DRLVaultV3, AnyswapWETHPermit, BlueberryProtocol, SwarmMarkets, WiseLending, ERC20TokenBank, Sushi_Router.

STRONGEST EVIDENCE OF EVOLUTION
1. Visibility gap (direct, Aug 2023+). Attacker txs seen in the public mempool before inclusion: 10 of 227 (4.4%) vs 86% of neighbouring
   txs in 2023 and about 50% in 2025-26. The 10 public ones were seen only 6-11 s before the block timestamp (median 8.9 s, under one slot)
   and mostly belong to unsophisticated attacks (WiseLending x2, Raft, kTAF, OrbitChain, Peapods, Particle, XBridge, Unilend, coinbase
   [row has an empty sources field, low confidence]). Public share by half-year: 2024H2 0/38, 2025H1 1/33, 2025H2 1/22, 2026 0/54.
2. Constructor shift. A bot that watches for a deploy and copies the trigger has nothing to copy when the exploit runs inside the CREATE.
   0% (2020-21), 6% (2022-23), 12% (2024), 52% (2025H1), 68% (2025H2), 37% (2026 YTD). The 2024 -> 2025 jump is outside the intervals.
3. The window closes. Median deploy-to-attack gap: 13-23 min (2020-22) -> 5 min (2023) -> 72 s (2025) -> 60 s (2026). Same-block deploy:
   0% -> 46% of own-contract attacks in 2025.
4. Gas competition stopped. Median priority fee 118 gwei (2021) -> 3 (2023) -> 1 (2024) -> 0.49 (2025) -> 0.1 (2026); median tx index 30-37 -> 8.5.
   Attackers no longer outbid anyone. (Ordinary tips fell too, so read with item 1.)
5. Bribes appear: direct coinbase payment 0% before 2022, 11% (2023), 16% (2024-26), including large ones (Makina 1,299 ETH, Hopelend 264 ETH,
   DeFiPlaza 66 ETH, Anyswap 34 ETH). Payers mostly have a near-zero tip, i.e. they buy builder priority rather than win a public gas auction.
6. What did not change: flash-loan use (35-60% in each year, no trend); unverified attack contracts (96-100%); sender/origin guards
   (25-50%, noisy, not monotone; a lower bound by our bytecode test).
7. chain scan medium/strong copycat candidates: 1/25 (Nomad), 1/89 (Kashi), 3/190 (RsETHSafeModule, FixedTokenBSwap, OMPxContract). None is a
   same-block-before copy. At about 1-4% per era there is no trend we can resolve: the visibility collapse says the opportunity shrank,
   but the candidate counts cannot yet show an effect on realised front-running.

EXTERNAL COMPARISON
- BACKRUNNER (arXiv 2409.06213): 17 of 158 attack txs visible in the public mempool, Jan 2023 - May 2024 (10.8%). Ours, exploit level, the
  overlapping measurable window Aug 2023 - May 2024: 8 of 75 public (10.7%). They agree. Their "bots intercepted 18/158" is not comparable to
  chain scan's candidates (they detect interception with their own bots/nodes; chain scan looks for copy txs within +/-1 block); chain scan finds 0 medium/strong
  candidates among the 109 exploits dated Jan 2023 - May 2024. BACKRUNNER saw 4 constructor attack txs in 1.5 years; we count 4 of 72 exploits
  in 2023 and 6 of 42 in 2024H1 (definitions differ: exploits vs txs).
- APE (Qin et al.): 10 of 12 whitehat attack txs private (83%). Our dumpster-era attackers are 95.6% private against a 50-86% control rate
  for ordinary txs, so private submission is the norm for adversaries too, not only a whitehat tactic.

CAVEATS
1. No direct exposure data before 2023-08-07. Pre-Aug-2023 labels are heuristic and were calibrated on the dumpster era (calibration.json):
   "zero priority fee" precision 98-100%, recall only 5-10%; "out-of-order" (later txs pay more yet this tx sits early) precision 82-89%,
   recall 3-6%. "Unknown" does NOT mean public: 43 of 51 E3 pre-Aug-2023 and 23 of 25 E2 exploits are unknown; only flagged ones count as
   private. Any E2/early-E3 "private share" would be an unsupported guess. E1 "public" is an era prior, not a measurement.
2. The dumpster drifted. Sources went from 7 (Oct 2023) to 3-4 (2024-25) and ordinary-tx visibility fell from about 86% to about 50%, partly
   real private flow, partly fewer sources (per-quarter summary files saved in calibration.json). Hence the comparison with same-block
   neighbours instead of absolute public shares. "Absent" means no source saw it before inclusion: Flashbots Protect, MEV Blocker, builder
   RPCs and bundles are not separable. Day 2024-10-29 is 404 upstream (one exploit, BUBAI, left unknown). Single-source "public" rows could
   be semi-private order flow (flagged as medium confidence).
3. Survivorship/selection: DeFiHackLabs only has exploits somebody wrote a PoC for; biased toward DeFi, 2023-26, and attacks worth reproducing.
   Yearly counts (7, 10, 32, 72, 81, 55, 54) partly track DeFiHackLabs coverage, not attack frequency. Exploits that were front-run away or
   unnoticed are missing; constructor attacks may be easier to notice.
4. One row per exploit uses the earliest listed tx that is not a pure deploy; multi-tx incidents carry all txs in all_txs. 9 exploits lost a
   listed c0ffeebabe tx by publication policy; where the rest is only the deploy (e.g. BiFi) the row shows created-in-this-tx, not constructor.
5. Guard detection is a lower bound: ORIGIN+EQ and hard-coded attacker addresses in bytecode. Storage-based owner checks are not separable
   from library callback checks (reported only as the weak flag caller_eq_weak). Constructor exploits have no callable surface and are
   excluded from guard rates. Own-contract attacks in 2025-26 are few (18, 25).
6. constructor_attack = root tx is a CREATE whose init code calls a listed victim or makes 3+ calls; the 2025-26 positives were eyeballed
   (dozens to thousands of inner calls each).
7. Tip medians are in gwei (pre-London = gas price). Small cells: E1 n=11; do not quote them without the interval.

CHART IDEAS FOR THE SITE
1. The gap opens: per quarter 2023Q3-2026Q4, "ordinary txs in the same blocks seen in the public mempool" (86% -> about 50%) vs "exploit txs
   seen" (11% -> 0%), dots sized by n. Data: by_era.json -> dumpster_era_by_quarter.
2. Where did the bots' window go: stacked bars by half-year of exploit style (constructor / internal CREATE / same-block deploy / earlier
   deploy), 2020-2026, annotated with Flashbots (2021), Merge (Sept 2022), and the 2025 jump.
3. Deploy-to-attack gap beeswarm by year, log-seconds axis with a line at 12 s (one slot); constructor exploits in a separate lane at zero.
   Data: per_exploit.json -> techniques.deploy_gap_s.
4. Gas stops mattering: priority fee vs block median tip (scatter, log scale, coloured by era) plus a lollipop for large coinbase bribes
   (Makina, Hopelend, DeFiPlaza).
5. Honesty strip: timeline band showing what is measurable per era (direct mempool Aug 2023+, heuristics only before) with "unknown" as a
   first-class colour. Optional: technique heat map by year (flash loan, bribe, origin check, unverified, multi-tx) showing what did not evolve.
